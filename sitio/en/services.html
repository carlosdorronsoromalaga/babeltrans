trans·la·tion

/trænsˈleɪʃ(ə)n/ n.

Translation is one of the arts that requires most dedication and talent, as the simplest text could be plagued with ambiguities.

  1. Commercial. web pages, offers, orders, brochures.
  2. Economic. reports, annual accounts.
  3. Legal. any law-related document.
  4. Technical. - scientific: engineering, automotion, technical manuals, assembly instructions, scientific.
  5. Medical. health care, pharmaceutical
  6. Sworn. deeds, (birth) certifiates, letters of authorization, degrees.

in·ter·pret·ing

/ɪnˈtɜːprɪtɪŋ/ n.

Interpreting is a complex activity that requires considerable effort and experience in order to obtain the best results.

  1. Consecutive. press conferences, corporate meetings, negotiations, commercial mediation.
  2. Simultaneous. seminars, meetings, conferences.
  3. Teleconference and videoconference. conference calls with partners, suppliers.
  4. Sworn interpretation. notary's office, courts, trials.
  5. Voice-overs. recordings in studios.
