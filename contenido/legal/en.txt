## What data we process
The data you give us when you write to us or fill in a form on this website: name, company, contact details, the details of your request and any documents you attach. For job applications, also the data in your CV.
## Why we use it and on what legal basis
- Quotes, enquiries and jobs: to answer your request, prepare a quote and, if you accept it, carry out the work. The legal basis is taking steps at your request before entering into a contract, and performing the contract (article 6.1.b GDPR).
- Documents for translation or interpreting: used only to provide the service ordered and handled in strict confidence.
- Job applications: to assess your profile as a freelance translator or interpreter. The legal basis is your consent (article 6.1.a GDPR), which you may withdraw at any time.
We do not make automated decisions or profile you, and we do not use your data for advertising.
## How long we keep it
- Requests that do not become a job: one year.
- Clients: for the duration of the relationship and then for the periods required by law (for example, six years for accounting records).
- Documents of a job: for as long as needed to carry it out and handle any revisions.
- Job applications: one year, unless you ask us to delete them sooner.
## Who receives it
We do not pass your data on to third parties unless required by law. Only the following have access, as far as necessary and under a confidentiality agreement:
- The translators and interpreters who carry out your job.
- Our web hosting and email provider ({{hosting}}), with servers {{servidores}}.
If a job requires a collaborator outside the European Economic Area, you will be told beforehand and the safeguards required by the GDPR will apply.
## Your rights

If you believe we have not handled your request properly, you may lodge a complaint with the Spanish Data Protection Agency (www.aepd.es).
